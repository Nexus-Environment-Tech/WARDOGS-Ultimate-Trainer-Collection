## 📋 Tactical Guide

### Understanding the Control Zone

The main mode is a **King of the Hill** variant where three teams compete for control of a randomized **2x2 km zone** on a 256 km² map:

- **Valkyra** vs **Lonestar** vs **Manticore**
- Every 30 seconds, the team with the most players in the zone earns **1 point**
- First team to **100 points** wins

### Cash Economy Strategy

- Start with **$10,000** per match
- Earn cash through: revives, transporting teammates, controlling objectives
- **Hot Zone** provides double cash
- Cash **persists between matches** — save or invest wisely

### Team Roles

- **Snipers** — ghillie suits for long-range elimination
- **Helicopter Pilots** — air support and supply drops
- **Engineers** — build forward operating bases and fortifications
- **Artillery** — bombard enemy positions from distance
- **Support** — revive teammates and transport supplies

### Pro Tips from the Battlefield

- **Never go on a mission alone** — staying close to teammates is the difference between losing equipment and recovering it with a timely revive
- **Take loose ammo, not full magazines** — pre-loaded magazines take more backpack space than stacked ammo
- **Carry a recon tool** — binoculars generate money and Recon XP while you use them
- **Don't rush every firefight** — maintaining cover and choosing the right moment to intervene is almost always better than attacking blindly
- **Manage your money wisely** — always keep some in reserve to avoid restarting with basic equipment after an unlucky death
